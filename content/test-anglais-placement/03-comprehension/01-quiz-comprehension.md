**Maya's move**

When Maya moved to a small town after living in the city all her life, she
expected to feel bored within weeks. To her surprise, the opposite happened.
Without the constant noise and rush, she began to notice things she had always
overlooked: the changing colours of the sky, the neighbours who actually
stopped to talk, the quiet that let her think.

At first she missed the convenience of the city — shops open late, food from
every corner of the world. But slowly she realised she had traded one kind of
richness for another. Six months in, a friend asked whether she regretted the
move. Maya paused. *"I thought I was giving something up,"* she said, *"but
really I was making room for something else."*
